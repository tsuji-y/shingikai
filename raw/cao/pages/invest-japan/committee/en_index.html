Task Force for Attracting Human and Financial Resources from Overseas

The Task Force for Attracting Human and Financial Resources from Overseas is newly established across ministries with the State-Ministerial level participation to follow up on the progress of the "Action Plan for Attracting Human and Financial Resources from Overseas" and identify issues and review institutional barriers with a view to attracting foreign human and financial resources.

Task Force for Promotion of Foreign Direct Investment in Japan

The Task Force for Promotion of Foreign Direct Investment which members are relative State Ministers is held with the purpose of managing the Investment Advisor Assignment System through which State Ministers etc. will act as advisors to companies that have made important investments in Japan in order to prepare an environment in which these companies can easily consult with the Japanese government, supporting the Council for Promotion of Foreign Direct Investment in Japan, and coordinating relative ministries and agencies.
