Summary of Recommendations from the “Expert Panel on Fundamental Reinforcement of
Defense Capabilities” (Sepetember 19, 2025) (1/2)

1. Overview of the Expert Panel

⚫ Based on the provision in the National Defense Strategy and related policies to establish a council for obtaining policy advice from experts in
order to strengthen the strategic and agile defense policy planning functions, the Expert Panel was established in February 2024 under the
Headquarters for the Realization of Fundamental Reinforcement of Defense Capabilities, headed by the Minister of Defense.

⚫ Members: Chairman SAKAKIBARA Sadayuki, Vice Chairman KITAOKA Shinichi, and 15 other members.

2. Summary of Recommendations

①Further Enhancement of Deterrence and Response Capabilities through Promotion of Seven Pillars of Fundamental
Reinforcement of Defense Capabilities and Introduction of Strategic Equipment

⚫ Amid the prolonged Russian invasion against Ukraine, the emergence of new way of warfare, and the further intensification of activities by
neighboring countries, it is an urgent priority to further strengthen deterrence and response capabilities, including counterstrike and sustained
combat capabilities. Strategic equipment such as unmanned assets, submarines equipped with Vertical Launching Systems (VLS), and defense
equipment that contributes to the establishment of a defense posture on the Pacific front should be considered for introduction. Regarding
submarines equipped with VLS, necessary research and technological development should be conducted, including consideration of utilizing next-
generation propulsion systems.

②More Sophisticated Equipment Procurement, Organizational Restructuring, and Transformation of Force Structure
that Directly Addresses Social Conditions

⚫ It is essential to face changes in social conditions such as inflation and population decline head-on, and to appropriately address matters including

the execution of the budget and the organizational structure of the JSDF.

⚫ Promote unmanned and manpower-saving operations through DX, unmanned assets, AI, and the use of external resources. Securing the

necessary personnel is GOJ’s responsibility.

⚫ Capabilities and force structure for the mid-2030s and beyond should be seriously considered, taking into account the future security environment

and demographic trends.

⚫ Upon organizational restructuring, it should be continuously reviewed including fundamentally reexamining intermediate commands and existing

units to ensure optimal operations, considering the establishment of JJOC and the advancement of AI and unmanned systems.

③Leading Enhancement of the Effectiveness of the Japan-U.S. Alliance from a Strategic Perspective and
Strengthening Cooperation with Like-Minded Countries

⚫ Japan should take the initiative in strengthening the Japan-U.S. security framework. Extended deterrence by the U.S.

is crucial.
Intergovernmental communication, including at the ministerial level, should be deepened, and concrete guidelines on extended deterrence should
be formulated. Consideration should also be given to how this will be explained to the public.

⚫ It is essential to strategically strengthen the coordination with countries in the Indo-Pacific. Additionally, it is necessary to promote cooperation

among defense authorities based on the spirit of OCEAN (One Cooperative Effort Among Nations: Perspective for the Indo-Pacific).

⚫ NATO is shifting toward focusing on defending Europe by themselves, and its involvement in the Indo-Pacific region may decline. Japan should

proactively pursue coordination with NATO in light of these strategic changes.

④Strategic Enhancement of Defense Technology and Production Base and Supply Chain;
Promotion of Expansion of Technological Development and Defense Equipment Transfers

⚫ There are limitations to independent development by small and medium-sized enterprises. It is necessary to consider various measures,
including promoting corporate consolidation and introducing state-owned arsenals. In light of the situation surrounding Russian invasion
against Ukraine, efforts should be also made to strengthen the domestic production system for essential equipment in times of contingency.

⚫ Regarding the stockpiling of ammunition and fuel, serious consideration should be made to secure a domestic supply chain for a certain
period. Additionally, cooperation with countries other than the U.S. in joint development and transfer of equipment should be pursued to
reinforce the supply chain.

⚫ Collaboration among industry, academia, and the government is crucial. An environment that facilitates the entry of startups, the adoption
of new technologies and equipment integration should be engendered. Furthermore, an environment should be established in which
researchers, including top scientists, can engage in defense-related research and development with peace of mind.

⚫ The transfer of defense equipment should be actively promoted. Although rules such as the so-called “five categories” have been
established, it is necessary to broaden the scope of transfers based on practical realities and with public understanding. One possible
approach is to allow unrestricted transfers to the countries that maintain friendly relationship with Japan, share values such as freedom and
democracy, and are under threat from other nations.

⑤Setting of Targets and Progress Management to Create a Virtuous Cycle Between
Defense Capability Reinforcement and Economic Growth

⚫ Defense and economy are not mutually exclusive; rather, a virtuous cycle between the two should be cultivated. It is important to recognize
that fundamental reinforcement of defense capabilities can contribute to solving economic challenges in Japan. Efforts should be made to
clearly communicate the public benefits associated with increased defense spending.

⚫ To promote collaboration between defense and industry, reference should be made to examples such as France’s defense public corporation

and its support for the defense industry through small-scale investments. Diverse models for securing funds should be considered.

⚫ The economic ripple effects of strengthening defense capabilities should be analyzed both qualitatively and quantitatively, and Evidence-

Based Policy Making (EBPM) should be promoted.

⑥Considerations for Further Fundamental Reinforcement of Defense Capabilities

⚫ Further enhancement of deterrence and response capabilities is an urgent challenge. Current measures should be steadily implemented,

and continuous efforts should be made to explore additional reinforcement strategies.

⚫ The “2% of GDP” benchmark is important as an expression of national intent. In pursuing further reinforcement of defense capabilities,
efforts should be made to ensure more efficient operations and cost reductions, while providing explanations supported by clear evidence.
In cases where sustained fiscal expenditure is required over the long term, securing stable financial resources to support such spending is
also essential.
